FAQ

answered.

The things people ask before starting an app project. If yours is not here, just ask.

What does an iOS app cost to build?

It depends almost entirely on scope, so we do not publish a headline number that would be wrong for you. A discovery sprint produces a fixed quote for the whole build before you commit to it, and that document is yours regardless of who ends up building the app.

How long does it take?

For a focused first version, usually three to five months from first conversation to App Store. Discovery and design take three to five weeks, the build six to twelve, then field testing and review on top.

Why native Swift instead of React Native or Flutter?

Cross-platform makes sense when you need Android and iOS on one budget and the app is mostly forms and lists. It costs you when the app leans on the device — location, camera, background behaviour, widgets — or when you want new iOS features on release day. If Android matters as much as iOS to you, we will say so and point you to a cross-platform team.

Do we need our own Apple Developer account?

Yes, in your company’s name. It is USD 99 a year. We will walk you through enrolment, including the D-U-N-S number Apple asks organisations for, and we publish under your account so the listing is never tied to us.

Who owns the code?

You do, on final payment — source, repository history and the App Store listing. No proprietary platform you have to keep paying to escape.

What if Apple rejects the app?

We handle it. Most rejections concern permission strings, account deletion or sign-in requirements, and we design around the common ones from the start. Submission is scheduled with a rejection budgeted in.

Do you build the backend as well?

Yes. Most business apps need one. If you already have an API we will build against it and tell you plainly if something in it will make the app worse.

Can you take over an app somebody else built?

Often. We start with a paid audit and give you an honest read: what is salvageable, what needs replacing, and the cost either way. Sometimes the answer is that a rebuild is cheaper than the rescue, and we will say so.

What does it cost to keep an app running?

Budget for an annual compatibility pass around each September iOS release, your Apple Developer membership, and backend hosting. Our Care Plan bundles the compatibility work with a monthly block of feature time.

Do you still do websites, SEO and AI automation?

Yes, for clients who want them — often alongside an app build. Apps are the focus of the studio now, but that work has not stopped.

Do you work outside Adelaide?

Anywhere in Australia. Discovery works best in person, so for interstate projects we usually travel for that week and run the rest remotely.

How do we start?

Send a short message describing who would use the app and what goes wrong today. We will reply within a business day and, if it looks like a fit, set up a call.

Still wondering?

Ask directly — you will get a straight answer, not a brochure.
