Proven — For Tilers

Crew hours, sites and safety

Proven runs the working day for tiling and trade crews: who is on site, what they are meant to be doing there, and the paperwork that has to follow them off it. Built native for iPhone and iPad, and live on the App Store.

  • Hours tied to where people actually wereA boundary is drawn around a site once. The app checks the worker is inside it before their hours start, so the timesheet is built from a checked position rather than from what somebody wrote down on Friday afternoon.
  • Drawings that say which revision is currentSheets are grouped by level and stamped with their revision. A superseded sheet stays visible but turns red, so nobody sets out a bathroom from the drawing that was replaced last Tuesday.
  • Waterproofing, step by stepEach package moves through its stages with photographs and flood test records attached, and finishes as a Certificate of Compliance to hand to the builder.
  • Safety documents with expiry datesToolbox talks, SWMS, safety data sheets and company policy sit on a shelf per job, each showing whether it is current, expiring or overdue.
