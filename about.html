About

A small studio
in Adelaide.

Lumen builds native iOS apps for businesses whose work happens away from a desk. We take on a few projects at a time, on purpose.

How we got here

Lumen started as a web and marketing studio for South Australian service businesses — websites, local search, and automations for tradies, clinics and firms around the northern suburbs. That work taught us something specific: the expensive problem for these businesses was almost never the website. It was the working day. Hours written on a scrap of paper. The drawing revision nobody told the crew about. The compliance certificate that had to be reconstructed from memory three months later.

Those are app problems, not website problems. So we moved. Today the studio is built around native iOS development, and the web, SEO and automation work continues alongside it for clients who want it.

What we believe

Ship it to a real phone early

A demo on a laptop proves nothing. Every two weeks you get a TestFlight build you can hand to the person who will actually use it. Opinions formed on a real device in real conditions are the only ones worth acting on.

Say no to the wrong project

If Android matters as much as iOS, or the idea is really a spreadsheet, we will tell you and point you elsewhere. A badly matched project helps nobody, and we would rather lose the work than deliver something we cannot stand behind.

You own everything

Source code, repository history and the App Store listing under your own developer account. No hostage situations, no proprietary platform you have to keep paying us to escape.

Accessibility is not a phase

Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and contrast get decided during design, where they cost nothing. Retrofitted afterwards they cost a rebuild, which is why most apps never do it.

Where we work

We are based in Adelaide and most of our clients are in South Australia, which means discovery can happen on site rather than over a call. We work with clients elsewhere in Australia too — for those we usually travel for the discovery week and run the rest remotely.
